HOW TO PLAY

Mac · keyboard + trackpad

Click the game to grab the pointer, then move to aim. Click or F fires; hold to fan the hammer. Two-finger click or SPACE: EAGLE EYE. C duck · R reload · SHIFT sprint · ESC pause.

iPhone · iPad · touch

Drag anywhere on the left to walk (push past the ring to run). Drag on the right to aim — aim assist helps. Hold FIRE to fan the hammer; you can aim while holding it. In EAGLE EYE, tap outlaws to paint marks.

  • Outlaws come from every side — windows, roofs, alleys. Red arrows at the edge point at shooters you can't see.
  • A bright glint means a gun is about to go off. Shoot first, or duck behind cover.
  • Six rounds in the cylinder. Reload behind cover; ducking while reloading keeps you alive.
  • Headshots, hat shots and quick kills build your combo multiplier. Keep it burning.
  • Hits fill the EAGLE EYE meter. Trigger it to slow time, paint up to six marks, and let the hammer fall.
  • Powder kegs marked with an X make a mighty fine mess.
  • Every stage ends in a duel. Hold steady… fire only on DRAW! Fire early and you lose.
